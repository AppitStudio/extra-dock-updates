VERSION: 4.4.16
DETAILS:

new: GIF widget: Put an animated GIF on your dock! Pick a GIF (or an animated PNG or WebP) from your Mac, or keep the built-in sample. A click can grow the GIF out of the dock, open an app or folder, play a sound or open a link. It has the same size and rounded corners as the app icons next to it, and it follows your file when you edit or replace it
new: Quick Look in the File Shelf: With the Shelf open, press Space to preview the selected file (or the one under the pointer), use the arrow keys to step through files, and press Space again to close the preview. Files also have a new "Quick Look" item in their right-click menu, and Escape closes the Shelf
new: Space Awareness app order: Sort the apps by name (A–Z or Z–A), by launch order, or in your own Custom order. Right-click an app to move it, or arrange the list in the widget's settings
new: Space Awareness "Minimize / Restore All Windows": A new left-click action. One click minimizes every window the app shows on the current Space, the next click brings that same set back
new: Space Awareness keeps hidden and minimized apps: Hidden and minimized apps stay in the widget, dimmed, on the Space they belong to. A new "Show apps with no windows" switch (off by default) also shows running apps that have no open windows, dimmed, on every Space
new: Open apps on the dock's screen: Shift-click an app in a dock to bring its window to that dock's screen. A new per-dock setting, "Open Apps on This Dock's Screen", does it on every click
new: Move the macOS Dock to the side: A new option under "Hide native macOS Dock" moves the hidden macOS Dock to a free side of the screen, so it can't pop up over your dock (for example in Mission Control). Your own Dock position and size come back when you turn it off. When your dock shares an edge with the macOS Dock, Settings › General also suggests it with a "Move to the Side" button
new: "Activate ExtraDock" prompt: Without an active license your docks stay on screen, but clicking, scrolling, dropping files or using a dock shortcut opens a small "Activate ExtraDock" window that explains why and shows the steps, with buttons to open License settings, get a license, or close it. Settings, the Manager and the menu bar keep working as usual
improved: The hidden macOS Dock stays hidden: If the macOS Dock comes back on screen even though ExtraDock is set to hide it (for example after a restart), ExtraDock notices and hides it again
improved: Trash updates right away: The Trash widget now updates within a second or two when you empty or move items that live in the Trash of another drive, instead of up to a minute later
improved: Dot badges: Apps that mark news with a dot or a word instead of a number (like Discord) now show a dot on their icon instead of a red "1"
improved: Smoother window restores: Bringing back a minimized window no longer freezes the dock for a moment while the window animates back
improved: Typing keeps working after using the Shelf: The keys you type go straight back to the app you were working in
improved: Blur and Liquid Glass explained: When macOS "Reduce Transparency" is on, a dock's Background setting now tells you why Blur or Liquid Glass looks solid
improved: Finder running dot: The Finder widget's running dot now looks and sits exactly like the dots under your other apps
improved: Keyboard shortcuts on every keyboard layout: ⌘A, ⌘C, ⌘V and the other shortcuts in App Stacks and the Manager now work with Russian, Greek, Hebrew and other non-Latin layouts
improved: Correct counts: Texts now read "1 item" and "1 dock" instead of "1 items" or "1 dock(s)"
improved: Export Logs includes full crash details: If ExtraDock ever crashes, the exported logs carry the complete crash report, so we can find and fix the cause faster
improved: Clearer Settings wording: The "Hide native macOS Dock" description now says that the macOS Dock stays hidden after you quit ExtraDock
bug fix: "Open License Settings" opens the License page: Opening Settings on a specific page no longer lands on the General page the first time
