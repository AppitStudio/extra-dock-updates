VERSION: 4.4.13
DETAILS:

new: Notch dock backgrounds: A notch dock now has a Background setting: Black, any Color, Blur, or Liquid Glass on macOS 26 and later (still marked Beta on macOS 27)
new: Hide docks from screen sharing: Settings → Behavior → Privacy keeps your docks out of Zoom, Meet and Teams shares, recordings and screenshots, while they stay on your screen. Thanks to Brandon for asking
new: A new color picker: Every color choice in ExtraDock now opens ExtraDock's own color picker instead of the macOS Colors window: 40 ready-made colors, your recent colors, a Custom tab with opacity, a hex field and an eyedropper
new: Label widget: A new widget that names a group of icons with a short title and an optional second line, in the width you choose, with vertical text for side docks
new: Tint icons: Appearance → Icon → Tint Icons colors every icon in a dock in one color
new: Always show names: Appearance → Icon → Always show names shows every item's name without hovering
new: Hide a dock item for a while: Click the eye button next to an item in the Manager to hide it without removing it. It comes back in the same place
new: One shortcut for several docks: Give docks the same Show/Hide shortcut and one press shows or hides them all together
new: One setting for every folder: Settings → General → Clicking a folder picks Browse beside dock or Open in Finder for all your folders at once
new: App Stack single click: A new App Stack setting opens apps and folders with one click
new: Shelf after dragging out: The Shelf settings have a new After Dragging Out choice: keep an item, or remove it once you drop it somewhere else. Thanks to Matan for the idea
improved: Safer Move to Trash: In App Stacks and the folder browser, Move to Trash… sits alone at the bottom, always asks first, and can be hidden in Settings → Behavior
improved: Folder browser closes like the macOS Dock: A pinned folder browser now closes when you click outside ExtraDock
improved: The same folder twice: Add a folder to a dock again, so one copy opens in Finder and the other browses beside the dock
improved: No duplicate Shelf items: Dropping a Shelf item back onto its own Shelf is refused, so you never get a second copy. Dropping it on the other Shelf, or dropping files from Finder, works as before
improved: Ready for macOS 27: Permission messages use "Device Control and Data Access", the new name for Accessibility on macOS 27
bug fix: Show/Hide shortcut on the first press: A dock's Show/Hide shortcut toggles once per press again, and a hidden dock shows on the first press
bug fix: Drop Shelf files on your dock apps: Dragging a file out of the Shelf panel onto an app in your dock opens it in that app, instead of landing on the Desktop
bug fix: No more blank icons in Live Dock: Apps you deleted while they were still pinned in the macOS Dock no longer show up as blank white icons. Reinstall an app and it comes back by itself
bug fix: Folder icons keep their settings: Changing a folder's icon no longer resets its Open With app and folder browser settings
