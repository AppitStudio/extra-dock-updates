VERSION: 4.2.1
DETAILS:

✨ new: A "Match macOS Dock" size preset lets you set your dock's icon size to exactly match your macOS Dock in one click.
✨ new: Right-click a dock and choose "Open Dock Settings" to jump straight to that dock's settings panel.
⚡ improved: Clicking a dock icon with "Normal" behavior now remembers which window you last used and returns you to it, instead of activating the app generically. If the app has no open windows, it reopens automatically.
⚡ improved: The right-click menu on docks has been completely redesigned — it now shows just the essentials (show/hide, position mode, size, copy/paste settings, and a link to full settings) instead of a long list of every option.
⚡ improved: Dock menus in the menu bar have been simplified too, with cleaner dock names and quicker access to the most important options.
⚡ improved: The show/hide toggle now correctly accounts for auto-hide, fullscreen hiding, and screen availability, so toggling visibility works reliably in all states.
⚡ improved: Screens that can't be reliably identified now show as "Identifying screen..." in the allowed-screens list and require confirmation before they can be assigned, preventing phantom screen entries.
⚡ improved: When two or more allowed screens are connected at the same time, a warning explains that a dock can only appear on one screen at a time and suggests creating separate docks instead.
⚡ improved: The dock list in the management screen now refreshes shortly after launch so visibility indicators are accurate right away.
⚡ improved: ExtraDock now automatically reports errors and crashes to help improve stability. You can opt out anytime in Settings > General > Privacy.
🐞 bug fix: Fixed clicking a dock icon not switching to the correct desktop Space when the app's window was on a different one.
🐞 bug fix: Fixed an issue where docks assigned to a specific screen could briefly appear on the wrong screen during sleep/wake transitions.
🐞 bug fix: Fixed the show/hide toggle sometimes getting out of sync when a dock was hidden due to its assigned screen being disconnected.
🎨 customization: The "Respect Dock Space" setting now displays a "Fixed docks only" badge to clarify it only applies to fixed-position docks.
🏷️ renamed: "Screen Assignments" is now called "Allowed Screens" in dock settings.
🗑️ removed: The "Create New Dock" and "Show Onboarding" options have been removed from the menu bar dropdown.
